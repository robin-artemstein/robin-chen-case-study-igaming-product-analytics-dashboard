Problem and challenge
  • Even though there are some similar services in the market, however, company need a proprietary dashboard for sensitive data.
  • Most of existing services has pretty steep learning curve, business users do not have much time to get used to it.
  • The UI design are not user-friendly for mobile users.
  • They complain that they can hardly read information from UI.
My approach
Color contrast comply WCAG level AA or above
Sortable UI cards and dark / light mode toggle
Optimize navigation bar for mobile users

Color contrast comply WCAG level AA or above
The contrast between background and foreground color is fundamental for accessibility design, it is particularly crucial for bussiness users.

So I take the WCAG compliance into the first consideration, user can read information is better than everything.

Sortable UI cards and dark / light mode toggle
Every user have his or her own difference working style, so I believe making UI highly customizable is necessary.

Users can sort UI card by drag and drop the title bar to wherever they want, and they can toggle from light mode to dark mode to pevent from eyes Streamlined and extend the battery life.


Optimize navigation bar for mobile users
According couple of journals in the UX design field, most mobile phone users usually place their thumbs on the bottom edge of the phone.

I believe that placing the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen on mobile devices would conform to the ergonomics of the thumb area and allow mobile users to use the product more comfortably.
