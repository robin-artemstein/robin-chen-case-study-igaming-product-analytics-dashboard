Overview
The client is a B2B iGaming corp, they aim to provide digital entertainment products for their vendors which are running business of betting or online casino. I worked closely with members at the product analytics team, to deliver a delightful and intuitive user experience for responsive dashboard application, to help vendors quantify players behavior and optimize their business.

Problem and challenge
  • Even though there are some similar services in the market, however, company need a proprietary dashboard for sensitive data.
  • Most of existing services has pretty steep learning curve, business users do not have much time to get used to it.
  • Many users are feeling uncomfortable when they are working on their smartphones.
  • Users are complaining that they can hardly read information from UI.
My approach
  • Requirements gathering
  • Information architecture
  • Color contrast comply with the WCAG level AA or above
  • Simple and intuitive UI card design
  • Highly customizable UI
  • Optimize navigation bar for mobile users
Requirements gathering
I worked closely with a product manager and UX developers to conduct a remote focus group user interview, we invited 10 vendor representatives in the Zoom sesson, to gather insights and understand what they need.
We synthesis these insights and crafted user personas for implementation plan formulation.
Information architecture
In order to integrate a smooth structure, I worked closely with teammates to run a card sorting, and organize the requirements.
This allow us to build information architecture, which is intuitive to navigate through without risking any confusion from the business users.
Color contrast comply with the WCAG level AA or above
The contrast between background and foreground color is fundamental for accessibility design, it is particularly crucial for bussiness users.
There are 35% of bussiness users complains that they sometimes need to make a lot of effort in order to read information from UI when they are using silimar products, because most of these products did not comply with the WCAG level AA or above.
This is why I decide to take the WCAG compliance into the first consideration, user can read information without making too many effor, this design can reduce the eye strain for users who need to work long hours.
Simple and intuitive UI card design
Most of generic product analytic dashboard applications in the market come with a lot of complicated terminologies, users are difficult to understand and they require a lot of time to learn, so this is a main pain point for business users which needs to be solved.
The first thing I decide to do is to discus this with the stakeholders at the company to understand the measurable metrics, synthesis the information, then create the UI.
The collected data after the initial release was launched showed that there are more than 45% of business users think that this design approach surely improved their onboarding experience.
Highly customizable UI
Every user have his or her own difference working style, so I believe making UI highly customizable is necessary.
Users can sort UI card by drag and drop the title bar to wherever they want, and users can toggle between light mode and dark mode.
Optimize navigation bar for mobile users
Business users sometimes need to use their smartphones to work out of their office, they reported that they feel pretty uncomfortable when they are using similar products for long hours.
I propose to place the navigation bar UI at the bottom of the screen on mobile devices will conform to the ergonomics of thumb area. This design will make mobile users use the product more comfortably as well.
The collected data after the initial release was launched showed that more than 60% of users think that this design can effectively improve their user experience on smartphones.
